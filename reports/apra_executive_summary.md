# APRA executive summary

## Observed facts

- Weighted risk in force increased from 1,862,717 in 2004 to 4,361,729 in 2024, a 134.2% increase.
- Gross earned premium increased from A$2.54bn to A$5.77bn over the same period.
- In 2024, reported claims were 43,014 (2.7% year on year) and gross incurred movements were A$2.78bn (6.7% year on year).
- Average incurred per reported claim was A$64,687 in 2024, up 3.9% from the prior reporting year.
- The aggregate finalisation ratio was 98.5% in 2024; this is a reporting-period ratio, not a cohort closure probability.
- Among recognised states with at least 100 reported claims, VIC had the highest 2024 incurred severity (A$75,760) and TAS the lowest (A$25,685).
- The selected claims basis contains 1,704 negative-payment rows and 28,969 negative-incurred rows; these were retained as adjustments.

## Interpretation

- Exposure grew 2.0% in the latest year while earned premium decreased 0.2%, which warrants segment-level investigation rather than an automatic pricing conclusion.
- Latest incurred growth exceeded reported-claim growth, with higher aggregate severity providing a descriptive claims-cost pressure signal.

## Scenario recommendations

- Use the claims-cost-index workbench to test severity, claim-count, inflation and margin assumptions. Outputs are illustrative portfolio scenarios, not production rate changes.

## Limitations

All findings use `claims_by_state_loi` or `policies_by_state_loi` as an explicit basis. Other APRA files are overlapping alternate cuts and are not added. The data is masked and aggregated; trends are descriptive and do not imply causation.
