# Limitations

- APRA data is masked and aggregated; it is not policyholder-level data.
- APRA report cuts overlap and cannot be added together.
- Negative financial movements may be legitimate adjustments and require business investigation.
- Descriptive trends do not establish causation.
- Development matrices are educational and are not production reserve indications.
- freMTPL2 represents French motor third-party liability experience, not the Australian market.
- GLM results demonstrate reproducible modelling practice, not production tariff adequacy.
- Simulated loadings omit many commercial, regulatory and operational considerations.
